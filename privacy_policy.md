# Privacy Policy

Dr.Math takes your privacy seriously. To better protect your privacy, we provide this privacy policy notice explaining the way your personal information is collected and used.

## Collection of Information

Dr.Math collects information necessary to provide you with a personalized learning experience and maintain your account. This information is provided by you during account creation and includes:
- **Personal Details:** Such as your name, email address, phone number, gender, and date of birth.
- **Location Context:** Such as your country, city, and region, to provide services relevant to your location.
- **Profile Photo:** If you choose to upload a logo or profile picture to personalize your account.
- **Usage Data:** Basic diagnostic and usage information collected for app performance, analytics, and maintenance purposes.

## Location Services

Our app requests access to your device's **Location Services (GPS)**. This information is used specifically to:
- Help students find nearby coaches and tutors.
- Provide an interactive map view showing the proximity of educational professionals in your area.
We do not use your precise location for advertising purposes or share it with third parties outside of the necessary service providers (like Google Maps SDK) required to show the map.

## In-App Purchases and Payment Processing

Dr.Math provides premium features through In-App Purchases (IAP). All financial transactions are processed securely through the official **Google Play In-App Billing** system. 
We do not directly collect, process, or store your credit card or payment details — all payment information is handled exclusively by Google Play under their own privacy policies.

## Notifications

This app uses **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)** to send notifications to users. These notifications may include educational updates, training reminders, or important account alerts. You can disable notifications at any time from your device settings.

## Third-Party SDKs

To provide specific technical features, we use trusted third-party SDKs:
- **Firebase:** For analytics, crash reporting, and notifications.
- **Google Maps SDK:** To provide the map interface and location-based coach searching.
These providers process data in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

## Security

The security of your personal information is important to us. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Changes To This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy is effective as of April 18, 2026, and will remain in effect except for any future updates, which will be effective immediately upon posting on this page.

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any significant changes will be communicated through an in-app notice or other appropriate means.
